## 3. Transport Latency & Unverified Boundaries Disclosure

1. **Security-vs-Latency Tradeoff (`CUL-SEC-002`):** Setting `direct_ws=False` (Playwright Pipe default) eliminates the unauthenticated loopback WebSocket port, increasing extreme `2,500`-element DOM discovery `p50` latency from `163.45 ms` (`0d19a9e`) to `367.09 ms` (`Pipe`), while standard forms stay at `9.46 ms` and `dense_90` at `25.26 ms`. Opting into `CUL_MCP_DIRECT_WS=1` recovers `152.43 ms` `dense_2500` `p50`.
2. **Untested External / Hardware Scopes:** Live LLM multi-turn benchmarks (`Track B1`), OS-level physical cursor/keyboard hook inspection outside `exebox`, live multi-tab public web embeds (`youtube.com`), and interactive `Calculator` Desktop `T2` remain explicitly open or **`NOT_RUN`**.
